What I do

I lead frontend architecture and public API design on Oracle JET, Oracle's open-source enterprise UI framework, which is supported by an internal developer community of more than 5,500 people. Much of the work is about longevity: designing component APIs that stay stable across releases, and deprecating old ones without breaking the applications built on them.

I also work across the full stack — backend services, observability, and AI-assisted development practices — and I care a lot about accessibility and mentoring the engineers around me.
